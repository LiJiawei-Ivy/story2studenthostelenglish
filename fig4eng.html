Notes

  • Private flat HK$17,000 is "rent per unit"; student accommodation and university hostels are "rent per bed".
  • University hostel is the estimated monthly rent per bed converted from the 2025/26 undergraduate hostel fees of the eight universities by room type: single approx. HK$4000, twin approx. HK$2500, triple approx. HK$1500, quad approx. HK$1,350 (excluding utilities, air-conditioning and meals; quad rooms are mainly at CUHK).
  • Each project has one color, and different room types are distinguished by the number of stacked bed icons (one layer = one bed; single = 1 bed, twin = 2 beds, and so on; only the top bed of the stack shows details, while the beds below are shown as neatly stacked mattresses). All room types of the same project share the same color. Rents are public quotes; a rent range for the same room type is shown with a solid bed (lower bound) and a hollow bed (upper bound) side by side; a single quote is shown with a solid bed only, without averaging; Yihui and Risun have the same quote for triple and quad rooms, so the two rows have the same value; Brim is the off-campus student hostel of CityU and HKBU in Kwai Chung.
Source: Public quotes from each project; the average monthly rent of a 431 sq ft private residential unit in Kowloon is HK$17,000 according to HKSAR Government data, cited by Centaline Property's Chen Yongjie in Ta Kung Pao (June 2026); hostel fees of the eight universities are 2025/26 figures published by each institution and compiled by Wen Wei Po.
