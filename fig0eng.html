Severe Bed Shortage in HK's Eight Public Universities: PolyU and HKU Coverage Below 20%

Total student population and bed ratio of major universities (2023/24 or 2024/25 academic year). The total length of the bar represents the total number of students, and the rust-colored portion represents the proportion of students who successfully obtained a bed.

Notes

  • "Beds" in the chart refer to on-campus student hostel beds, excluding off-campus accommodation or public/private housing.
  • Student numbers and bed counts are for the 2023/24 or 2024/25 academic year, rounded to the nearest hundred.
  • Bed shortage is the difference between the number of beds and the total number of students. Due to rounding in the statistical tables, the difference may not exactly equal the sum of the above numbers.
  • Sorted by total student population in descending order.
Source: Midland IC&I Research Department | Graphic: Yu Ziqi
Caption: A 100% stacked horizontal bar chart showing the total student population and bed ratio of the eight public universities (2023/24 or 2024/25 academic year). The chart shows that PolyU and HKU have the lowest bed coverage rates, both below 20%, while CUHK and Lingnan have the highest coverage rates at around 45%. Data also shows that HKU and PolyU have the most severe bed shortages, with shortfalls of approximately 34,500 and 23,800 beds respectively.
