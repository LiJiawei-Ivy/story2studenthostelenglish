Non-local Students Surge 45.5% in Two Years, Target 100000 by 2029/30

Non-local students in locally accredited full-time post-secondary programmes, including UGC-funded and self-financing programmes. The cap rose to 40% from 2024/25 and will further rise to 50% from 2026/27.

Notes

  • This chart does not include the policy cap percentage; 79300 is the territory-wide figure, not the non-local student count for the eight universities, and cannot be used to infer whether the cap is fully utilized.
  • Official complete data for the 2025/26 to 2028/29 academic years are unavailable; these years are left blank in the chart.
  • The 100000 target for 2029/30 is set in the 2024 Policy Address and is not an official forecast.
Source: EDB submission to LegCo, 2024 Policy Address | Graphic: Yu Ziqi
